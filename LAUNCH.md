# Launch CareerMatch: your first 20 users

## Do these first

1. In `config.js`, add your contact link. If you create a Google Form for feedback, add its link as `feedbackUrl`.
2. Upload the website files to GitHub Pages and test the report with at least three different résumés.
3. Share the same link with 20 people who are actively applying for jobs. Ask every person for one specific improvement.
4. Keep a simple list: name, where they found the site, whether they completed a report, and their feedback. Do not store their résumé.

## LinkedIn post (copy and edit)

> I built CareerMatch, a free tool that compares your résumé with a job description and gives you a skills-match report, gaps to prepare for, and interview questions.
>
> Your résumé stays in your browser — no account needed.
>
> I’m looking for 20 job seekers to try it and share honest feedback: [YOUR WEBSITE LINK]

## WhatsApp / college group message

> I made a free résumé and interview-prep tool. Upload your résumé, paste a job description, and get a skills match, missing skills, and interview questions. Your file stays private in your browser. Try it here: [YOUR WEBSITE LINK]
>
> Please send me one suggestion after trying it.

## Short video plan

Record a 20–30 second screen recording:

1. Show the headline.
2. Upload a sample résumé.
3. Paste a sample job description.
4. Show the match score and missing-skills cards.
5. End with the website link and: “Try it free. No account needed.”

## Google Search Console

After the site is public, add the exact GitHub Pages URL to Google Search Console, verify ownership, then use URL Inspection to request indexing of the homepage. Search visibility takes time; make useful role-specific content later rather than relying on keywords alone.
