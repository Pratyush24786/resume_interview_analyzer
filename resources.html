Which CareerMatch tool should you use?

The best tool depends on what you are trying to check. If you want to review your resume for ATS-oriented preparation, start with the ATS resume checker.

If you already have a specific job posting, use the resume job description matcher to compare your resume with that particular role.

If you specifically want to review relevant skills and terms, use the resume keyword checker .

You can then use the results to decide what parts of your application deserve further review.

Why compare your resume with a job description?

A resume can contain strong experience while still failing to communicate the most relevant parts of that experience for a particular position.

A job description gives you information about the skills, technologies, qualifications, responsibilities, and experience that the employer is looking for.

Comparing your resume with that information can help you identify areas where your genuine experience could be communicated more clearly.

Should you add every keyword from a job description?

No.

Only include skills and qualifications that accurately represent your experience.

If a job description contains a technology or skill that you do not have, do not add it simply to improve a matching result. Instead, treat it as information about a potential skill gap.

How to improve your resume after checking it

Make relevant experience easy to find

If you genuinely have experience with a skill required for the role, make that experience clear in your resume.

Use clear section headings

Sections such as Experience, Education, Skills, Projects, and Certifications can make important information easier to locate.

Describe achievements

Whenever possible, explain what you accomplished rather than only listing responsibilities. Accurate numbers and outcomes can make your contribution clearer.

Tailor your application

Different jobs have different priorities. Review the specific job description before submitting your application rather than relying on exactly the same resume for every role.

Preparing for the interview

Resume preparation is only one part of the job-search process. Once you have identified the important requirements of a role, use them to guide your interview preparation.

Practise explaining your genuine experience, projects, technical skills, decisions, challenges, and results.

Use the CareerMatch interview question generator to practise before your interview.

Important: match results are not hiring guarantees

A resume match result should be treated as a preparation aid, not as a guarantee of an interview or job offer.

There is no universal ATS score that applies to every employer. Different organisations can use different recruitment systems and hiring processes.

CareerMatch provides a simplified analysis to help you review your application. It does not claim to reproduce the private scoring system of a particular employer.
