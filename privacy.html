PRIVACY

Your résumé stays private.

CareerMatch reads the résumé and job description in your browser to create the report. This website does not upload, save, sell, or send the résumé or job description to its owner.

What may be collected

If you choose to open a feedback, contact, booking, or payment link, that external service may collect information under its own privacy policy. This website also uses public libraries to read PDF and DOCX files; the files themselves are not sent to those libraries.

Before you use this site

Do not include passwords, financial information, government identity numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive information in a résumé you upload anywhere online. The report is an educational preparation tool and is not a hiring guarantee.
