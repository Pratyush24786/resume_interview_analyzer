RÉSUMÉ GUIDE

Six ways to make your résumé more convincing.

A good résumé is easy to scan and gives clear proof of what you can do. Use these improvements before every application.

1. Start with the target role

Use a clear role title or short summary near the top. Match it to the job you want instead of using vague phrases such as “hardworking student.”

2. Show outcomes, not only duties

Replace “Responsible for social media” with a result: “Planned 12 posts per month and increased engagement by 25%.” Use numbers only when they are true.

3. Make skills easy to find

Include a concise skills section. Use names employers recognise, such as Python, SQL, Excel, React, communication, or project management.

4. Tailor it to each job

Read the job description and emphasise the projects and skills that are most relevant. Use the résumé matcher to find likely gaps.

5. Keep formatting simple

Use readable headings, consistent dates, and enough whitespace. Avoid graphics-heavy templates that can hide important text from résumé parsers.

6. Prepare to explain every line

An interviewer can ask about anything on the page. Keep only skills and achievements you can explain with a real example.
