What is an ATS resume checker?

An applicant tracking system, commonly called an ATS, is software that organisations may use to help manage job applications and candidate information. Resume documents can be processed and searched for information relevant to a job.

An ATS resume checker can help you review whether the information in your resume is clearly presented and relevant to a particular job description.

CareerMatch provides a simple preparation tool that compares recognised skills and relevant terms between your resume and the job description you provide.

Why should you check your resume against the job description?

A resume can be strong in general but still fail to communicate the most relevant experience for a particular position.

Comparing your resume with the actual job description can help you notice important skills, technologies, qualifications, or areas of experience that you should review.

The goal should not be to add every keyword you see. Instead, make sure your resume clearly communicates skills and experience that you genuinely have.

How to make a resume more ATS-friendly

Use clear section headings

Use familiar headings such as Experience, Education, Skills, Projects, and Certifications where appropriate. Clear structure can make a resume easier for both people and software to interpret.

Keep formatting readable

Use consistent fonts, spacing, dates, and bullet points. Avoid unnecessarily complicated layouts that make important information difficult to identify.

Use relevant terminology

When you genuinely have a skill mentioned in a job description, make sure your experience with that skill is described clearly. Do not claim skills you do not have.

Describe achievements

Whenever possible, explain what you accomplished rather than only listing your responsibilities. Accurate numbers and measurable outcomes can make achievements easier to understand.

Tailor each application

Different jobs can prioritise different skills. Review the specific job description before submitting your resume rather than relying on one completely generic version for every application.

Does an ATS resume checker guarantee an interview?

No. A resume checker cannot guarantee that an employer will contact you or offer you an interview.

Hiring decisions can depend on many factors, including qualifications, experience, communication, interview performance, competition, and the requirements of the organisation.

CareerMatch is intended as a preparation and resume-review tool, not as a prediction of hiring decisions.

Is the CareerMatch score an official ATS score?

No. CareerMatch does not claim to reproduce the proprietary scoring system of any particular employer or applicant tracking system.

Its matching result is a simplified comparison of recognised skills and terms between the resume and job description you provide. Use the result as one source of feedback when improving your application.
