      UNDERSTAND YOUR RESULTS

      What this résumé match can—and cannot—tell you.

      A comparison, not a hiring score

      The match is based on skill and keyword overlap in the text you provide. It does not predict whether an employer or an applicant tracking system will select you.

      Review every suggestion

      A term may be missing because your résumé describes the experience differently. Add details only when they accurately represent your background.

      Text-based files work best

      Scanned or image-only PDFs may not contain readable text. Use a text-based PDF or DOCX for the best chance of extracting résumé content.
