        A CLEARER WAY TO PREPARE

        What a resume match can—and cannot—tell you

        See relevant overlap

        CareerMatch compares readable resume text with the skills it recognizes in the job description, then gives you a starting point for review.

        Use the score as a guide

        The percentage reflects skill overlap in the text you provide. It is not a hiring prediction and does not represent a score from an employer’s ATS.

        Keep your claims accurate

        Review every suggested keyword. Add it to your resume only when it reflects experience you can explain truthfully.

        HOW THE REPORT IS BUILT

        A transparent skills comparison, not an AI hiring score.

        CareerMatch uses a defined list of common skills and checks for those terms in the readable text of your résumé and job description. The report is a practical review aid you can verify yourself.

        1. Extract readable textPDF, DOCX, and TXT files are read in your browser. Scanned image-only PDFs may not provide text to compare.
        2. Find recognized skillsSkill names are matched without case sensitivity. The tool does not understand synonyms, context, proficiency, or years of experience.
        3. Calculate overlapThe percentage is matched recognized job-description skills divided by all recognized skills in that description. It is not an employer ATS score or a prediction of interview success.

        Use the results as a checklist. Confirm each finding and include a skill on your résumé only when your experience supports it. Read the privacy details.

        QUICK ANSWERS

        Resume analyzer questions

        Which resume file types can I analyze?

        Upload a PDF, DOCX, or TXT resume up to 5 MB. Text-based PDFs work best; scanned image-only PDFs may not contain readable text.

        Does CareerMatch upload or store my resume?

        The analysis runs in your browser. The website does not upload or store your resume file.

        Does the match score predict whether I will get hired?

        No. It estimates overlap between recognized skills in your resume and the job description. Employers use different criteria, and the score is not an ATS result or hiring prediction.
