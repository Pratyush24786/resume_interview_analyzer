      Know what the score means

      A useful comparison, not a hiring decision.

      CareerMatch uses text and skill matching to help you review an application. It cannot predict an employer’s or ATS’s decision.

      Use your own judgement

      • Verify every suggested skill against your experience.
      • Do not add keywords for skills you do not have.
      • Describe achievements accurately.
      • Use a text-based PDF or DOCX when possible.

      Common questions

      About the free resume analyzer

      Does CareerMatch upload or store my resume?

      No. The analysis runs in your browser; the resume file is not uploaded, saved, or sent by this website.

      Does the match score predict an ATS result?

      No. It reflects text and skill overlap in the documents you provide. It cannot predict an employer’s decision.

      Can it read a scanned PDF?

      Scanned PDFs may contain images instead of selectable text. Use a text-based PDF, DOCX, or TXT file for better text extraction.
