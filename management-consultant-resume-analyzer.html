What hiring teams scan for

Use the language of the job description where it is truthful: strategy, market analysis, operating model, transformation, financial analysis, stakeholder management, and executive communication. Prioritise the business problem and result over vague responsibility statements.

Achievement bullet framework

Led [workstream] for [client, team, or business unit], synthesizing [inputs] into [recommendation or implementation] and delivering [verified financial, operational, or customer result].

Interview preparation

Prepare concise STAR stories for leading without authority, structuring an ambiguous problem, resolving a stakeholder conflict, and recovering from an unsuccessful recommendation. Practise explaining the insight and the decision separately.

Formatting checklist

Make employer, role, location, and dates immediately readable. Use bullets with strong verbs and specific scale. Keep a single, consistent date format and make sure essential content is selectable text.
