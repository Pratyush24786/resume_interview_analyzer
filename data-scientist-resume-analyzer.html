ROLE-SPECIFIC RÉSUMÉ GUIDE

Data Scientist Résumé Review

Show how you turned data into a trustworthy decision. Compare your résumé to the target role with CareerMatch, then refine every claim with evidence.

Analyze my résumé →

Keywords that matter when relevant

Reflect the actual role: SQL, Python, experimentation, statistics, dashboards, machine learning, data pipelines, stakeholder communication, and the product or business domain. Do not add tools you have not used.

Achievement bullet framework

Analyzed [dataset or product area] with [method], identifying [insight] that informed [decision] and changed [verified business or user metric] by [result]. Include data quality checks and your contribution.

Interview preparation

Prepare a data-quality example, an experiment or evaluation decision, a case where you explained results to non-specialists, and a project where the first approach did not work.

Formatting checklist

Use plain headings such as Experience, Projects, Education, and Skills. Describe models with context and outcomes, not just tool names. Keep charts and portfolios supplemental rather than your only evidence.
