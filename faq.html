FAQ

CareerMatch questions, answered.

Is CareerMatch free?

Yes. The tools and guides on this website are free to use.

Is my résumé uploaded?

No. The analyser reads the file in your browser. It does not upload or store the résumé or job description.

Is the match score an ATS score?

No. It is a simple comparison of recognised skills in your résumé and the job description. It is a preparation aid, not a hiring prediction.

Why does a PDF not work?

Scanned or image-only PDFs do not contain selectable text. Use a text-based PDF, DOCX, or TXT version, or convert the scan with OCR first.

Should I add missing skills to my résumé?

Only add a skill when you have genuinely learned or used it. Instead of claiming a missing skill, explain how you are building it through a course, project, or practice.

How should I use the interview questions?

Prepare short, true stories using the STAR method. Focus on your actions and measurable results.
